# Explain the product to a client

“Upload your spreadsheet, choose an email template, review your recipients, and send through your Microsoft account.”

## A safe first demonstration

Creation uses one five-step navigation: **Upload → Email setup → Choose templates → Personalize → Save spreadsheet**. Email setup selects the address column, not individual recipients. Connect all required personalization columns before saving; blank row values can be corrected afterward. Subject options are under Email setup. The save summary shows the source file, worksheet, address column, template selection, and connected fields. Back preserves your choices, including returning to Upload and continuing with the same file.

1. On Dashboard, choose **Upload spreadsheet**. Open the quick guide and download the fictional sample Excel file.
2. Upload it. Choose **Public Email** as the recipient address column. Explain that this is where the email goes, not text inside the message.
3. Choose **One email for everyone**, or use **Industry** for different messages. Review the choices. If nothing matches exactly, choose a message or skip those recipients.
4. Connect **company_name** to **Business Name**. Say: “The email says Hi company name. Your spreadsheet supplies Northstar Labs, so that is what the recipient sees.” Connect any other fields used by the chosen email.
5. Choose **Save and select recipients**. Nothing has been sent. Select the recipients and explain the difference between **this page** and **all matching**.
6. Choose **Review and send**. Open a row to show the finished email. Point out From, To, email template, missing details, duplicate policy, and practice mode.
7. Confirm **Practice**. No real email is sent. Keep the browser tab open until results appear, then show the saved message in **Send history**.

## Choosing and editing email templates

Open **Template library** to search by name, industry, or subject. **Ready-made templates** are starting points: preview one, then choose **Make a copy** to customize it. **My templates** shows your own messages. Archived messages are hidden until you choose **Archived**; copy, archive, restore, and delete are under **More options**.

In the editor, enter an internal name, write the subject and message, and check the live **Sample email preview**. Its sample values are not saved or sent. **Add your own field** inserts a field such as `{{decision_maker}}`; you can also type fields directly. Connect those fields to spreadsheet columns during spreadsheet setup. The signature comes from Settings, not a spreadsheet column.

Category and description, plain-text alternatives, and availability are optional settings. Save your changes, then choose the template in your spreadsheet. Review actual recipient previews there before sending.

## Revisiting a saved spreadsheet

The details page has one navigation with five steps: **Email setup → Choose templates → Personalize → Select recipients → Review & send**. It opens at the first unfinished step; saved setups usually open at Select recipients. The small Saved indicators describe saved configuration, not whether an email was sent. File information is under Spreadsheet details, not another tab. Back returns to the previous step. Unsaved edits prompt before changing steps, and missing setup links directly to the step that needs attention.

## When they are ready to send real emails

Settings uses five top tabs, showing one section at a time: **Email account**, **Signature**, **Sending**, **My account**, and **Data & privacy**. The Email account tab connects the sending mailbox; My account separates workspace details from login-password controls; neither changes the From address. Unfinished edits remain when switching tabs. Save account and sending preferences before leaving; signature text and formatting use Save signature. Add and select a block to edit it in the focused editor; the preview shows unsaved edits. New blocks, uploaded logos, ordering, and deletion apply immediately. Connected mailboxes hide technical setup until Edit connection setup is selected. Speed and file retention are under Advanced settings. The Do not email list blocks opt-out addresses; deleting application data is a separate, collapsed action with explicit confirmation.

In Settings, connect the intended Microsoft mailbox with help from their administrator. Build an optional signature, including a logo if wanted. Enable real sending deliberately. Start with one controlled test address, review the actual From and To accounts, and confirm.

Email account also offers **Send real test email**: preview the fixed dummy message, enter an inbox you control, and confirm a real send. It sends even in practice mode without enabling real campaigns. The message appears in Microsoft Sent Items, not campaign history. Uncertain results require checking Sent Items before a deliberate retry. **Test connection** remains a separate check that sends no email.

The signature block list scrolls independently, with the selected editor alongside it on desktop. Formatting groups appearance separately from link behavior. My account also has a separate **Change password** form that checks the current login password, verifies matching new passwords, and updates the login password only. It does not change your Microsoft password or workspace contact email.

“Accepted by Microsoft” means Microsoft accepted the request, not guaranteed delivery. If an outcome is unconfirmed, check Sent Items before resolving it; never assume it is safe to resend.
